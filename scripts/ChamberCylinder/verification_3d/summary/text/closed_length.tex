The isotropic tube was run with 2, 4, 6 and 8 times the
test-case length (Table~\ref{tab:closed-length}). In the $2L_0$ tube the mid-length plane
lies $4.1H$ from the plate, inside the end layer: its axial strain is 17\% off at nr = 2
and still 15\% at nr = 4, and $\rho$ does not converge either. From $4L_0$ on the
differences are those of the mesh. The $6L_0$ and $8L_0$ tubes agree to three digits
($\rho$ $4.59\cdot10^{-3}$ for both at nr = 2, $\varepsilon$ $2.04\cdot10^{-2}$ and
$2.05\cdot10^{-2}$), so $6L_0$ is long enough.
