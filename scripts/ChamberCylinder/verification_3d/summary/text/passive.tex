Three walls, all with circumferential fibers or none, so the eighth model applies:
an isotropic neo-Hookean wall ($\mu = 100$~kPa), the same wall with one circumferential
fiber family (Holzapfel-Ogden, $a = 100$~kPa, $b = 0$, $a_f = 100$~kPa, $b_f = 1$, no
tension-compression switch), and the full Holzapfel-Ogden law with all four terms and the
switch ($a = 50$~kPa, $b = 5$, $a_f = 25$~kPa, $b_f = 20$, $a_s = 25$~kPa, $b_s = 5$,
$a_{fs} = 10$~kPa, $b_{fs} = 5$, $k = 100$).

All kinematic quantities converge to the 0D block: the volume at third to fourth order
(nodal fibers and quadratic elements on the exact cylindrical surfaces), the
through-wall displacement and the wall thickness at 2.5--3, the strains, the Jacobian and
the stresses at about 1.5--2. The full Holzapfel-Ogden wall was refined to nr = 16
(148896 elements, 10~h on two cluster cores): volume $7.6\cdot10^{-8}$ (order 3.8), $\rho$
$5.5\cdot10^{-6}$ (2.9), strains and stresses $1\cdot10^{-4}$--$7\cdot10^{-4}$ (1.3--1.9). Against the
best estimate of the converged 0D solution instead of the reference the volume difference
is the same to three digits. The largest field errors sit at the endocardium. The nodal
hydrostatic stress oscillates between vertex and mid-edge node levels (penalty
$\kappa(J-1)$), so $J$ and $\sigma_{rr}$ are compared as element means.
