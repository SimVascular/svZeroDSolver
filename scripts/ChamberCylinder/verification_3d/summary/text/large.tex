With half the shear modulus ($\mu = 50$~kPa) the isotropic tube nearly doubles its volume
at 120~mmHg (+96\%), at 58\% of the limit pressure $\mu\ln(R_e/R_i)$ that an
incompressible neo-Hookean tube approaches asymptotically. The large deformation does not
change the picture: the volume converges at orders 3.7 and 3.5 to $3.7\cdot10^{-5}$ at
nr = 4, the radial displacement at 2.9 and 2.5 to $2.0\cdot10^{-4}$. The volume differences
are within 3\% of those of the stiffer isotropic case (Table~\ref{tab:passive_isotropic-kin}),
the radial-displacement differences 2.4 times smaller relative to the larger displacement. Softer still, the 0D
block stops at the limit pressure (no equilibrium exists beyond it under pressure
control): at $\mu = 30$ and 24~kPa its solver fails at 93\% and 96\% of $\mu\ln(R_e/R_i)$.
