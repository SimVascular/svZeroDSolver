Table~\ref{tab:direct-ref} repeats the 3D comparisons with the direct ne = 256 (passive) or
ne = 128 (active) 0D solution as the reference (\texttt{--reference-ne}). Up to nr = 4 the
3D differences do not change in the digits shown. At nr = 8 the volume difference moves
by the error of the direct solution itself (anisotropic wall: $1.47\cdot10^{-6}$ with the
extrapolated reference, $1.40\cdot10^{-6}$ with ne = 256), which is why the extrapolated
reference is kept. At nr = 16 (full Holzapfel-Ogden wall) the direct reference would
misreport the volume difference: $2.3\cdot10^{-7}$ instead of $7.6\cdot10^{-8}$, an apparent
order of 2.1 instead of 3.8. Against the best estimate (Richardson 128/256) the volume
difference is $7.6\cdot10^{-8}$ as with the 32/64 reference.
