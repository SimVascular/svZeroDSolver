A uniform $30^\circ$ helix has no cancellation of fiber torques and twists the 0D tube by
$-6.9^\circ$ over $2L_0$ at 120~mmHg, the largest twist of the helix-angle sweep in the
appendix figure. At the middle of the $2L_0$ tube the twist rate converges to the 0D value
at about fourth order ($3.8\cdot10^{-2}$ at nr = 1, $2.9\cdot10^{-3}$ at nr = 2), the volume
and the radial displacement at about third order. The torsion of the 0D block is
therefore right, and the slow convergence of the $\pm60^\circ$ twist rate is the
cancellation, not a model difference.
