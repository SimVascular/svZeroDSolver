The $\pm60^\circ$ helix combines a transmurally varying fiber angle with a small twist;
the uniform $30^\circ$ helix a large twist with a constant angle. A helix from $+60^\circ$
at the endocardium to $0^\circ$ at the epicardium combines a varying angle with a large
twist ($-5.6^\circ$ over $2L_0$ at 120~mmHg). At the middle of the tube all kinematic
quantities converge to the 0D block: the twist rate at about fourth order
($3.1\cdot10^{-3}$ at nr = 1, $1.9\cdot10^{-4}$ at nr = 2), the volume and the radial
displacement at third order, the transmural rotation at 2.5.
