\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Ends of helical tubes.} The uniform 0D state is that of an infinitely long
tube; a finite 3D tube with ends that only keep $u_z = 0$ deviates from it near the ends.
Comparing the middle of a tube twice as long removes this from the passive comparison. A 3D end
condition that reproduces the 0D section shear would need 0D data in the 3D model, which
this verification avoids on purpose; svMultiPhysics has no twist-periodic condition
that would make the tube infinite.
\item \textbf{Coupled helical case.} The circulation sees the volume of the whole 3D
tube, end layers included, so the end effect also changes the pressure and, through it,
the state at mid-length. A longer tube shrinks the share of the end layers but costs
proportionally more; the 0D replay (Section~\ref{sec:replay}) separates the two parts.
\item \textbf{Small twist of the $\pm60^\circ$ helix.} The net twist is a small
difference of the torques of the two fiber layers, so its relative error is the stress
error amplified by this cancellation. It converges with the mesh, but late: in the middle
of the $2L_0$ tube 15\%, 8\% and 1.6\% at nr = 1, 2, 4. The uniform $30^\circ$ helix shows
the twist without the cancellation.
\item \textbf{Time integration.} The remaining pressure and valve-flow differences of the
coupled circumferential case are the difference between the coupled 3D (generalized-$\alpha$
with the implicit 0D coupling) and the stand-alone 0D time integration. They fall with
the time step, but not quite at second order (2.6$\times$ for half the step).
\item \textbf{Not covered.} The 3D model reproduces the 0D block with fixed length,
with free length for passive inflation with circumferential fibers (end plate,
Section~\ref{sec:closed}), without wall inertia and viscosity, and with a prescribed
active tension. Free length with helical fibers or in the circulation, inertia, viscosity
and the BCS active law of the 0D block have no 3D counterpart in this verification.
\item \textbf{Solvers.} Without a direct solver, the full cylinder relies on Trilinos ML,
whose quality falls with the number of MPI ranks. The largest cases (full cylinder at
nr = 8, longer tubes at nr = 4) therefore run on one or two ranks for many hours. For a
tube four times as long at nr = 4 (76k elements) ML no longer reduced the linear residual.
\end{itemize}
