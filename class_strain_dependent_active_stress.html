Detailed Description

Strain-dependent active stress model from [1].

Owns 8 extra equations and 7 extra variables ( , in that order):

with the strain in fiber direction (a function of the chamber's radius), and evolution equations

and the additional outputs (sarcomere stress, series element stress and strain)

where (activation strain-dependence) and (relaxation strain-dependence) are piecewise-linear functions of , given as break point tables (n0_e_c/n0_values and m0_e_c/m0_values) and evaluated with linear_interpolate, and is a reaction-rate signal supplied by the chamber's ActivationFunction (e.g. PiecewiseRateActivation).

Parameters: E_s, mu, alpha_r, alpha, k_0, sigma_0, n0_e_c, n0_values, m0_e_c, m0_values
